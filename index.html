How to play

  1. Roll the dice (or flip the cards).
  2. Read the story starter out loud.
  3. Tell a story that uses every picture. Speak for one minute, or write 5–8 sentences.
  4. The gold ✏️ grammar die tells you which grammar to use in your story. Press ⚙ to choose which grammar options can appear.
  5. Playing with a partner? Take turns: each person adds one sentence using the next picture.
  6. Too easy? Use 7 or 9 dice, hide the English words, and add a past tense to every sentence.
  7. Teaching a class? Press ⛶ Present for a fullscreen view with images only — everyone interprets the pictures their own way.
